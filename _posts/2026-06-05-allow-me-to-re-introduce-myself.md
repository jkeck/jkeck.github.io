The last post on this blog is dated May 2020. A lot has changed.

When I stopped writing, I was a software engineer focused on the craft. The kind of content that lives here reflected that. Technical explorations, things I was trying to learn by forcing myself to write about them.  Then a pandemic hit (well my last post was definitely pandemic induced).

Since then: a new company, a move from IC to Engineering Manager, and an industry that looks almost unrecognizable compared to six years ago. I'm still keeping my hands in the code, but the problems I'm sitting with day-to-day are different. Team dynamics, quality bars, how you ship responsibly in a domain where mistakes carry real weight. Like everyone else in this industry right now I'm figuring out what AI actually changes, and what it just makes louder.

That's mostly what this blog will be about going forward. Not takes, exactly. More like working through things I have strong intuitions about but haven't fully pressure-tested yet. Some of it will be about managing and leading engineering teams. Some will be specific to building AI features in high-stakes domains. Some will probably surprise me as I write it.

The site got a refresh. The writing is back. Good to be here.
